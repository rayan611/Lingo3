## 2026-11-26 — Reading Library: stories from the word bank (v0.8.0)

**New tab `/library` ("Reading Library") in Training Center.** Swedish stories written ahead of time from the 5000-word Swedish bank, at A1, A2, B1, B2 and C1, in five kinds (story, fact, life, funny, information). Several levels can be chosen at once; a story at a level also uses the levels beneath it. The list scrolls, each row has a tick box (kept on the device in `localStorage`), and **New story** opens an unread one from the chosen levels. In a story, every word is tappable: it shows English and German from the bank, the inflected forms, an example and audio, and **+ add to my words** sends it through the existing `addRelatedWord` path (nothing is added without that tap). An English toggle shows each paragraph's translation. **Data:** `public/reading/vocab.json` (5080 entries: 5000 from the bank plus 80 from the owner's own workbook), `public/reading/stories/{a1,a2,b1,b2,c1}.json` (20 stories, 160-230 words each), `public/reading/index.json` (lists the story files); fetched on demand, not bundled. **Tools:** `scripts/build-reading-vocab.py` rebuilds the vocabulary from the Excel files; `scripts/reading-coverage.py` reports per-story level mix and how many bank words the stories use (`--unused B1 60` lists words no story uses yet, to steer the next batch); `scripts/check-reading.ts` joined `npm test`. **Facts about the source workbook that shape this:** it holds Swedish for 5000 words only (A1 1000, A2 1000, B1 1500, B2 1500); the 3000 C1 rows are English-only, so C1 stories use B2 vocabulary with harder grammar and structure; German exists for A1 and A2 only (2000 words), so B1 and above show no German in the tap card; the bank's "A1" is a workplace list, so everyday words such as *katt* or *kaffe* sit at A2/B1 and A1 stories unavoidably include some of them. **Coverage today:** 20 stories use about 42% of A1, 17% of A2, 6% of B1 and 4% of B2 words; reaching all 5000 needs a few hundred stories, written in batches with `--unused`. **Not built:** German-language stories (the format supports it), word search over the full bank, syncing ticks across devices, offline caching of the JSON beyond the browser cache. Tested: `npm test`, build; not tested on a real phone.
