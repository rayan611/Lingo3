Levels & placement

Start at the right level

Languages use CEFR A1–C2 by default. The architecture keeps levels configurable so future subjects can use their own frameworks.

A1–A2

Foundation

Everyday basics, essential grammar, practical vocabulary and guided conversation.

B1–B2

Independent

More fluent conversation, work/study situations, structured writing and stronger comprehension.

C1–C2

Advanced

Nuance, professional communication and high-level academic or exam-oriented language.

Placement test: planned for the Learning Tools phase. It will recommend a level and course; no fake result is generated today.
